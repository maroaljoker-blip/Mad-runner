# Mad-runner
Mad-runner is a game focusing on fighting enemies while running as you face 4 diffrent enemies in each section



Gameplay

You'll fight different enemies throughout the game, and each one has its own mechanics.

- Skeleton – A basic enemy that's good for learning the combat.
- Red Skeleton – you can only kill by heavy attack
- Blue Skeleton – you can only kill by light attack

At the end of the game, you'll face the final boss. It has its own attack patterns and special mechanics, so defeating it isn't just about attacking as fast as you can. You'll need to time your attacks, dodge at the right moments

Controls

- E – Light Attack
- Q – Heavy Attack
- Space – Jump
- S or Down Arrow – Roll

Cheats

I added a few cheats at the beginning of the game so anyone can enjoy it, even if they're not really into difficult games. They're also useful for testing.

- High Jump – Jump much higher than normal.
- Speed – Move faster.
